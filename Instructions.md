
# Service Blurb Writing Instructions

## Overview
Rewrite all service blurbs in `Service_Blurbs.xlsx` to be conversion-focused and structured for automated parsing. Current blurbs are too long (300+ words) and lack clear structure. Target is 200-300 characters with specific formatting.

---

## Required Format (Must Follow Exactly)

```
[One-line value proposition]. • [Benefit 1] • [Benefit 2] • [Benefit 3] • [Trust signal with number]. [Emotional closing].
```

**Critical:** Use bullet separators (•) between benefits - these enable automatic parsing.

---

## Mandatory Criteria

### ✅ MUST HAVE:

1. **Length:** 200-300 characters total (not words)
2. **Structure:** 5 distinct parts separated by periods
3. **Bullet separator:** Use • (Option+8 on Mac, Alt+0149 on Windows) between benefits
4. **At least ONE number:** Customer count, countries, years, rating, or dollar amount
5. **Specific metrics:** "in 10 minutes" not "quickly", "$4.50" not "affordable"

### ❌ MUST REMOVE:

1. **No "we recommend"** or "we suggest" phrases
2. **No repetition** of service name or category
3. **No generic adjectives** without specifics: "great", "good", "excellent"
4. **No long paragraphs** - keep to single sentences
5. **No "often"** or "complex" without explaining what

---

## 5-Part Formula

### Part 1: Value Proposition (10-15 words)
**What it does + main benefit + specific outcome**

✅ Good:
- "Send money to 170+ countries in minutes with guaranteed delivery"
- "Multi-currency account with real exchange rates and transparent fees"
- "Incorporate your Canadian business in 10 minutes online"

❌ Bad:
- "We recommend this service because it's great"
- "A digital remittance service that helps users"
- "Provides quality financial services"

### Part 2-4: Benefits (3 benefits with • separators)
**Each benefit: 5-10 words, specific and actionable**

✅ Good:
- "• Bank deposits, cash pickups, and mobile wallets available"
- "• Coverage from $1,000 to $15,000 per year"
- "• 24/7 emergency assistance in 40+ languages"

❌ Bad:
- "• Good customer service"
- "• Reliable and trustworthy"
- "• Makes things easier"

### Part 3: Trust Signal (MUST include a number)
**Pick ONE of these patterns:**

- "Trusted by [X]M+ customers worldwide"
- "Available in [X]+ countries"
- "Rated [X]/5 stars with [X]+ reviews"
- "[X] years in business"
- "Backed by [Company Name]"
- "FDIC-insured up to $[X]"

### Part 4: Emotional Close (5-10 words)
**Tie to relocation journey**

✅ Good:
- "Stay financially connected during your move"
- "Build your Canadian banking foundation from day one"
- "Keep your furry family members protected too"
- "Get on the road in 48 hours"

❌ Bad:
- "We're here to help you"
- "Contact us for more information"
- "Sign up today"

## Quick Examples by Category

### Banking/Money Transfer
```
Send money to [X]+ countries in minutes with [low fees/guaranteed delivery]. • [Delivery options] • [Fee structure] • [Features]. Trusted by [X]M+ customers. [Stay connected during relocation].
```

**Example:**
```
Send money to 170+ countries in minutes with guaranteed delivery. • Bank deposits, cash pickups, and mobile wallets available • Low fees and competitive exchange rates • Trusted by 5M+ customers worldwide. Stay financially connected during your move.
```

### Insurance
```
[Type] insurance covering [what] for [duration/amount]. • Coverage from $[X] to $[Y] • [Key feature] • [Support details]. [Trusted metric]. [Protection statement].
```

**Example:**
```
Comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost luggage. • 24/7 emergency assistance in 40+ languages • Coverage from $25 for short trips • Plans for individuals and families. Travel with confidence during your relocation.
```

### Phone/Data Plans
```
[Service type] in [X]+ countries with [benefit]. • No [pain point] • Plans from $[X] • [Installation time/process]. [Avoid problem statement].
```

**Example:**
```
Stay connected in 200+ countries with affordable eSIM data plans. • No physical SIM card needed • Plans from $4.50 • Install and activate in minutes. Avoid expensive roaming charges from day one.
```

### Vehicle Services
```
[Rental/purchase/lease] for [target audience] with [unique benefit]. • [No barrier] • [Support included] • [Delivery/timing]. [Get mobile quickly].
```

**Example:**
```
Vehicle rentals and leases designed for newcomers without local credit history. • Short and long-term options available • Insurance and registration assistance included • Delivery to your door. Get on the road in 48 hours.
```

## Step-by-Step Process

### For Each Service:

1. **Research the service** - look up actual stats (customers, countries, years)
2. **Find the main benefit** - what problem does it solve for newcomers?
3. **List 3 specific features** - what makes it different?
4. **Get a number** - customer count, rating, coverage, or savings
5. **Write using the formula** - assemble all 5 parts
6. **Count characters** - must be 200-300 (use character counter online)
7. **Check for •** - ensure bullet separator between benefits
8. **Verify number** - must have at least ONE metric with digits

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Too Long
```
Remitly is a digital remittance service that enables users to send money internationally quickly, affordably, and securely, primarily, to family and friends. We recommend Remitly for its fast transfers, multiple payout options, and reliable customer support. With Remitly, you can choose how your money is delivered...
```
*Problem: 340+ characters, repetitive, lacks structure*

### ✅ Correct Length
```
Send money to 170+ countries in minutes with guaranteed delivery. • Bank deposits, cash pickups, and mobile wallets available • Low fees and competitive exchange rates • Trusted by 5M+ customers worldwide. Stay financially connected during your move.
```
*Solution: 245 characters, clear structure, includes numbers*

## Where to Find Numbers

If service website doesn't list statistics, use:
- **Trustpilot/Reviews:** Customer count, star ratings
- **About page:** Years in business, team size
- **Coverage page:** Countries, locations, cities
- **Pricing page:** Specific dollar amounts, savings percentages
- **Wikipedia:** Founding year, customer metrics

If absolutely no numbers available, use:
- "Industry-leading [service type]"
- "Professional-grade coverage"
- But TRY HARD to find at least one metric

## Batch Processing Tips

### Efficient Workflow:
1. Sort services by category (all banking together, all insurance together)
2. Write one perfect example per category
3. Use that as template for similar services
4. Just swap specific details (numbers, features, names)
5. Review all blurbs in category at once for consistency
